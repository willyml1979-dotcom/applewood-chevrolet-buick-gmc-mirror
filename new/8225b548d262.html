The 2025 GMC Sierra 3500HD SLT, in an elegant gray finish, is a robust truck designed for those seeking a reliable 4x4 drivetrain combined with a new level of performance and luxury. With an automatic transmission and powered by a Duramax 6.6L Turbo-Diesel V8 engine, this powerhouse offers an impressive 470 horsepower at 2800 RPM, ensuring you can tackle any task with ease.

Externally, this Crew Cab Standard Box model features 20" bright face wheels with dark painted pockets, all-terrain blackwall tires, and the SLT Premium Package including chrome assist steps and a spray-on bedliner that exudes both style and functionality. Inside, the black leather seats offer a comfortable and stylish ride with heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, and an inviting cabin enhanced by a power sunroof.

The Sierra's technological capabilities are sure to impress, boasting a backup camera, navigation system, bluetooth connection, and smart device integration, all accessible while you enjoy the tunes on your satellite radio. Safety and driver assistance features such as lane assist, cruise control, and the Sierra HD Pro Safety Plus Package ensure peace of mind during every drive.
